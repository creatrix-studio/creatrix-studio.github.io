HostLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Publisher: Creatrix (크리에이트릭스) · Contact: creatrix.studio26@gmail.com

Summary

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • Your network data stays on your phone. IP addresses, device names, scan results, DNS results and your history are processed and stored only on your device.
  • To improve the app we collect anonymous usage events (Google Analytics for Firebase and our own server) and crash and performance reports (Sentry). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks first and collects nothing until you agree. Elsewhere both are on by default; to have processing stopped, write to creatrix.studio26@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

HostLens reads your device's network configuration (local IP address, subnet, router address, DNS servers) and sends network requests on your local network and to public test hosts (for example apple.com and 1.1.1.1) to measure reachability and response times. Results, including devices found on your network, are kept in the app's storage on your phone and are deleted when you delete them or the app. We do not receive them.

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The app sends anonymous product events such as 'check-up completed', 'paywall shown' and 'purchase completed'. It also sets a few properties from fixed lists: device region, app language, purchase status, whether notifications are allowed and the week the app was first opened. Firebase also processes an app instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version, language and an approximate country derived from your connection. Events never include IP addresses, hostnames, device names, UPnP or SNMP fields, DNS results, ranges or scan data. We disabled advertising features and do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android). Data is retained for 2 months. Provider: Google LLC (United States). Some of these events, such as a failed scan and its reason or a purchase, are also sent to our own server so we can count them reliably. The server receives only the event name with its fixed values, the time, the app and OS versions and the platform. It stores no IP address and no user or device identifier; it uses your IP address only in memory, to limit repeated sending. Events are deleted after 1 year. The server is hosted on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and the data is stored by Supabase, Inc. in the United States. Legal basis in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: your consent.

Crash and performance reports (Sentry)

The app sends crash, freeze and performance reports: a stack trace, device model, OS and app version, whether a session ended normally, how long app start and screens take, and a trail of recent screens and app events. Requests to our own server are recorded with their path, status and duration; requests on your network and to test hosts are never recorded. Before sending, the app removes network data and free-form context from the report, including IP addresses, hostnames, device names, UPnP and SNMP fields, DNS results, ranges and scan data, and the service is configured not to store IP addresses. Reports are retained for up to 90 days. Provider: Functional Software, Inc. d/b/a Sentry (United States). Legal basis in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: your consent.

Speed test and router pages

The speed test opens FAST.com, a service by Netflix, in an in-app browser; it measures your speed under its own privacy policy and HostLens does not receive the results. "Open router settings" opens your router's own web page on your local network.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, the app sends your message, the topic you chose, the app and OS versions and the app language. No account, email address, device ID, or network or device data is sent, and no ID is created for you. The data is sent over an encrypted connection when you tap Send, processed by our server (hosted on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure) and stored by Supabase, Inc. in the United States, solely to read your feedback and improve the app. Our server uses your IP address only in memory, to limit repeated sending, and does not store it with your feedback. We delete feedback after 1 year. Because nothing links a message to you, we cannot reply to it or find it later on request. If you do not want this transfer, do not send feedback; the app keeps working fully. Legal basis in the EEA/UK: our legitimate interest in improving the app.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). On iOS, every feature is included in the paid app. On Android, a one-time purchase unlocks HostLens Pro. We never see your payment details.

Transfers abroad (notice for Korea, Japan and other regions)

Anonymous usage events and crash reports are sent over an encrypted connection while the app is in use, and are stored in the United States by the providers named above, solely to measure and improve the app, for the retention periods stated above. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland nothing is sent unless you agree. Elsewhere you can refuse the transfer by writing to creatrix.studio26@gmail.com; the app keeps working fully.

Retention and deletion

Data on your phone is kept until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Usage events and crash reports are deleted automatically at the end of the retention periods above. Feedback is deleted from our database after 1 year. We keep no other copy.

Children

HostLens is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and rights

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy, and collection stops right away. Elsewhere you can ask us to stop processing by writing to creatrix.studio26@gmail.com; we reply within 10 days. Because the data we receive is not linked to your identity, we usually cannot identify it as yours. For any other request or question, contact the same address and we will help as far as possible.

Contact

Privacy contact: Creatrix (크리에이트릭스), creatrix.studio26@gmail.com. We reply within 10 days.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and show the new effective date above.
