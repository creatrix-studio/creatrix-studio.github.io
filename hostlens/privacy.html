HostLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Publisher: Creatrix (크리에이트릭스) · Contact: creatrix.studio26@gmail.com

Summary

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • Your network data stays on your iPhone. IP addresses, device names, scan results, DNS results and your history are processed and stored only on your device.
  • To improve the app we collect anonymous usage events (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Sentry). You can turn both off in Settings → Privacy. Usage analytics is never enabled for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland.

Data that stays on your device

HostLens reads your device's network configuration (local IP address, subnet, router address, DNS servers) and sends network requests on your local network and to public test hosts (for example apple.com and 1.1.1.1) to measure reachability and response times. Results, including devices found on your network, are kept in the app's storage on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete them or the app. We do not receive them.

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

When enabled, the app sends anonymous events such as "check-up completed", "scan completed" (with a rough device-count range), "paywall shown" and "purchase completed". Firebase also processes an app instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version, language and an approximate country derived from your connection. Events never include IP addresses, device names, hostnames, DNS results or scan results. We disabled advertising features and do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Data is retained for 2 months. Provider: Google LLC (United States).

Crash reports (Sentry)

When enabled, the app sends crash and freeze reports containing technical information (stack trace, device model, OS and app version). Before sending, the app removes IP addresses and hostnames from the report, and the service is configured not to store IP addresses. Reports are retained for up to 90 days. Provider: Functional Software, Inc. d/b/a Sentry (United States). Legal basis in the EEA/UK: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

Speed test and router pages

The speed test opens FAST.com, a service by Netflix, in an in-app browser; it measures your speed under its own privacy policy and HostLens does not receive the results. "Open router settings" opens your router's own web page on your local network.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). On iOS, every feature is included in the paid app. On Android, a one-time purchase unlocks HostLens Pro. We never see your payment details.

Transfers abroad (notice for Korea, Japan and other regions)

Anonymous usage events and crash reports are transferred to and stored in the United States by the providers named above, solely to measure and improve the app, for the retention periods stated above. You can refuse by turning off "Usage analytics" and "Crash reports" in Settings → Privacy; the app keeps working fully.

Children

HostLens is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off usage analytics and crash reports at any time in Settings → Privacy. Because the data we receive is not linked to your identity, we usually cannot identify it as yours; if you have a request or question, contact creatrix.studio26@gmail.com and we will help as far as possible.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and show the new effective date above.
