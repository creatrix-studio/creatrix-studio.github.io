Frequently asked questions

Why can't I see some devices?

Some devices don't answer while asleep or block discovery. Guest networks and "client isolation" also hide devices from each other. Wake the device and scan again.

What does "No response" mean?

The device didn't answer in time. It may still be online but ignoring pings or that port.

Why does a scan show only my router?

Many routers isolate devices on guest Wi‑Fi, so scans can only see the router. Connect to your main network and try again.

Why are results different with a VPN?

With a VPN or iCloud Private Relay on, internet and DNS tests go through the tunnel, so response times look different.

HostLens can't find anything at all

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure HostLens is turned on.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open HostLens → Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Google account you used to buy it.
