RackNote

See last time, log every set.

RackNote is a gym workout log that shows last time's weight beside every set. No sign-up is needed, and your workout log stays on your device.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, purchase help or privacy requests, email creatrix.studio26+racknote@gmail.com. Include your phone model, operating system and app version when you report a problem. We reply within 10 days.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my workout log stored?

On your device only. RackNote has no account and does not upload your sets, routines or history. To keep a copy, open Settings → Data and save a backup file or export as CSV.

Can I bring my log from another app?

Yes. RackNote imports CSV exports from Strong, Hevy and JEFIT, and CSV files exported from RackNote. The import screen shows how to export from each app. You can undo an import in one step from Settings → Data.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings → Data → Save backup file and keep the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, choose Restore from backup when RackNote starts.

What is free, and what does Lifetime add?

Logging, import, export, backups, next-weight prefill and up to 2 routines are free. Lifetime is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited routines and per-exercise trend charts. It is not renewed or billed again. Your log is never locked if you stay free.

How do I restore Lifetime?

Open the Lifetime screen and tap Restore purchase at the bottom. Use the same Apple Account or Google Play account you bought with, and connect to the internet. A purchase does not move between Apple and Google.

How do refunds work?

Apple and Google handle purchases and refunds under their own policies and applicable consumer law. Request an Apple refund at Report a Problem, or follow Google Play's refund instructions.

Can I turn off usage statistics?

Yes. Open Settings → Privacy and turn off the statistics switch. RackNote then deletes the statistics it has not sent yet and sends no more. Crash reports are always sent because we need them to fix the app; they contain no workout log.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a workout or a routine from its own screen. To remove everything, delete the app; on Android you can also clear its storage. Export or back up first if you want to keep your log. For data held by our providers, see the privacy policy or email us.
