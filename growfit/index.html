GrowFit

A gym coach that picks your next weight and reps

GrowFit is a gym progression coach: you log your workouts, and it plans your next sessions and suggests how to progress from what you logged.

You can also bring in past workouts from a file exported by another workout app.

Your records are stored on your device, so you can use GrowFit without an account.

GrowFit is a training aid and does not give medical advice.

Contact

We reply within 10 days, and requests about your personal data are answered within the legal time limits set out in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my workout records stored?

On your device only. GrowFit has no account or cloud sync, so Creatrix does not receive your workout records.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, use Export backup file in Settings and keep the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, use Import backup file in Settings. On Android, GrowFit’s data is not included in Google’s cloud backup or device transfer, so keep your own backup file.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a workout from its own screen, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. Deleting the app does not delete files you have exported. For data held by our providers, see the privacy policy or email us.

Can I turn off usage statistics?

Yes: turn off “App usage stats” in Settings. Crash reports are always sent because we need them to fix the app, and they contain no workout records.

How do I restore my subscription?

Use the restore option in the app while signed in to the same store account you bought with. A subscription does not move between Apple and Google.

How do I cancel?

Cancel in Apple’s subscription settings (https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions) or Google Play’s (https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do refunds work?

Apple decides refunds for App Store purchases (https://reportaproblem.apple.com), and Google decides refunds for Google Play purchases (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). If Google Play or a store sends you to us, email creatrix.studio26+growfit@gmail.com and we will work with you.

What happens when my subscription ends?

You can still view and export your history.
