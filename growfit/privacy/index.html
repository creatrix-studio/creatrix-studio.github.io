Privacy Policy

GrowFit · Effective date: 2026-11-26

Scope

In this policy, we lay out: what data we collect and why; how your data is handled; and your rights with respect to your data.

Credit card information is submitted directly to Apple or Google and doesn’t hit Creatrix servers.

On-device records

GrowFit stores these records on your device: workout sessions and dates, exercises including ones you create, sets, weight lifted, reps, reps in reserve, routines, progression inputs and results, imported workouts, your training preferences and whether your subscription is active.

We use these records only on your device to log your workouts, show your history and plan your next workouts.

GrowFit has no Creatrix account and does not sync these records to Creatrix servers, so Creatrix does not receive them.

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, and the app only reads whether your subscription is active from the store.

If you import a CSV file exported from another app, the app reads it on your device.

If you import a routine from a photo on iPhone, the app reads the text in the photo you pick on your device and does not send the photo.

If you export your records or share a backup file, the file is saved or sent where you choose, outside the app.

Workout data and location

Some workout data you enter, such as weights, reps and reps in reserve, may count as health-related information under some laws.

This data stays on your device and is not included in crash reports or usage analytics.

The app uses it only on your device to provide the features you use, and never for advertising.

GrowFit does not use Apple HealthKit or Android Health Connect.

GrowFit does not ask for location permission and does not collect GPS or other location data.

The app reads your device’s region settings to choose default units and to decide whether to ask for analytics consent.

On Android, it can also use the country of your SIM card or mobile network for that consent decision.

We do not process your data for advertising: the app collects no advertising ID and sends nothing for ad measurement.

The analytics consent screen shown in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland also asks about ads, but that answer is only saved and has no effect, because the app does no advertising processing.

On iPhone, the app may ask for Apple’s tracking permission, but whatever you answer, the app does no advertising processing and shares no data with advertising companies.

Crash reporting

When GrowFit crashes or stops responding, the app sends a crash report to Sentry, our crash reporting provider (Functional Software, Inc., doing business as Sentry, in the United States).

A crash report contains the crash or hang stack trace with personal details removed, your device model, OS and app version, and a fixed set of labels for the app’s recent steps.

The app also tells Sentry each time an app session starts and whether it ended normally or crashed, together with a random installation ID that Sentry’s software creates on your device.

This installation ID stays the same until you delete and reinstall the app or clear its data, so sessions from the same installation can be linked, but it is not linked to your name, email address or store account, and the app does not show it.

We use session information to see how stable each release is, and it is always sent, in every region, with no switch.

Crash reports and session information contain no health data, no workout records and nothing you type, and they include no screenshots or screen recordings.

We collect crash reports and session information in every region on the basis of our legitimate interest in finding and fixing errors (GDPR Article 6(1)(f); Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act, Article 15(1)(6)), not on your consent.

You can object to this processing at any time by emailing creatrix.studio26+growfit@gmail.com (Personal Information Protection Act, Article 37; GDPR Article 21).

We review your objection and, if you ask, delete the reports we have already received that we can find (see “Your privacy rights” for how we find them).

Because the app has no switch for crash reports, the only way to stop new reports from your device is to delete the app.

Usage analytics

GrowFit sends a small number of usage events to Creatrix’s own analytics server.

Today these are an event when onboarding starts and an event when onboarding is completed.

Each event contains a random event ID, the event name and time, a fixed app label, the platform, and the app and OS versions.

Events contain no health data, no workout records, nothing you type and no ID that stays the same across events.

In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, based on your device’s region settings, the app sends usage events only after you agree.

Elsewhere, including Korea, usage analytics are on by default on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f) where it applies; Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act, Article 15(1)(6)), not on consent.

You can object or withdraw at any time by turning off “App usage stats” in Settings or by emailing creatrix.studio26+growfit@gmail.com (Personal Information Protection Act, Article 37; GDPR Article 21).

Turning it off stops new events and clears events still waiting on your device, but it does not recall events already sent.

Events waiting to be sent are kept on your device for at most 30 days and 500 events, and they are not included in device backups.

Turning off usage analytics does not change crash reporting or any app feature.

Usage events are stored in the United States, and the network edge that first receives them, with the other recipients and countries, is described under “Recipients and international transfers”.

Recipients and international transfers

We use some third-party subprocessors to help run our applications and provide the app to you.

The app sends personal data abroad only to the recipients below, and for each one this section gives the information that Article 28-8 of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act requires.

We transfer this data abroad only as far as Article 28-8(1) of that Act allows, and only to run the app as this policy describes.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., doing business as Sentry), United States, privacy contact https://sentry.io/privacy/: receives the crash reports and session information described under “Crash reporting”, including the installation ID, over HTTPS when a crash or session happens or, after a crash, usually the next time the app opens, and keeps them for at most 90 days.

To refuse the transfer to Sentry, email us; what happens then is described under “Crash reporting”.

Creatrix’s analytics server, operated by Creatrix (contact creatrix.studio26+growfit@gmail.com) on Google Cloud in Oregon, United States: receives the usage events described under “Usage analytics” over HTTPS when an event is recorded and when the app opens or returns to the foreground, uses your IP address only in memory to limit request rates, and deletes event records one year after receiving them.

Google Cloud, provided to us by Google Cloud Korea LLC, with the servers run by Google LLC in Oregon, United States, privacy notice https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice: hosts the analytics server and passes each usage-event request to it when the request is sent.

Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), United States, privacy contact privacyquestions@cloudflare.com: receives each usage-event upload over HTTPS at a Cloudflare data center, usually the one nearest to you, together with connection details such as your IP address, and forwards it to the analytics server through an encrypted tunnel.

Supabase (Supabase Pte Ltd, Singapore), with the database stored on Amazon Web Services in Oregon, United States, privacy contact privacy@supabase.io: stores the usage-event records that the analytics server writes over a certificate-verified connection, until they are deleted one year after receipt.

To refuse the usage-event transfers, turn off “App usage stats” in Settings, decline where the app asks, or email us.

After you refuse, no new events are sent, but events already sent are not recalled or deleted, and the app’s features and crash reports keep working.

Apple and Google process your purchases under their own privacy policies when you buy through their stores.

If you email us, your message is handled by Gmail, a service of Google LLC in the United States, because our support address is a Gmail address.

Our web pages, including this policy, are hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., in the United States, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Korea, where Creatrix is based, is covered by adequacy decisions of the European Commission and the United Kingdom.

When personal data of users in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland is transferred to the United States, Sentry, Google, Cloudflare and GitHub are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss–US Data Privacy Framework (checked on 26 November 2026), and Supabase’s data processing agreement includes the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum.

You can check the certifications at https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov, and you can ask us for a copy of the standard contractual clauses.

We have not named a representative in the EU or the UK, and if the law requires one, we will name it on this page.

Until then, you can write to us directly at creatrix.studio26+growfit@gmail.com.

Retention and deletion

Workout records stay on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app.

Removing the app does not delete files you exported or shared, or your purchase history with Apple or Google.

On Android, GrowFit leaves all app data out of cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

On iOS, your device backups, such as iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer, may include the app’s records.

Sentry keeps crash reports and session information for at most 90 days.

Our analytics server deletes usage event records one year after receiving them, through a daily deletion job.

The analytics server’s system log records each request’s path, result and duration without your IP address, and it has a size limit, so the oldest entries are removed as new ones arrive.

Deleted event records can remain in Supabase’s database backups until those backups expire under Supabase’s service terms.

Cloudflare keeps connection logs for the time its own privacy policy sets, and we see only aggregated traffic and error statistics.

We keep support emails for 1 year after we receive them and then delete them.

Security

Your workout records are kept in the app’s storage on your device and are not sent to Creatrix.

Before a crash report is sent, filters in the app remove user and request details and any free text.

Usage events are sent over HTTPS and carry no ID that stays the same across events.

Our analytics server writes event records to its database over a certificate-verified connection.

The analytics server accepts no direct connections from the internet: requests reach it only through Cloudflare’s encrypted tunnel, and administrators sign in only through Google Cloud’s Identity-Aware Proxy with short-lived keys.

The database password and other server secrets are kept in a secrets manager, not in the app or in its code.

Only Creatrix can access the analytics server, its database and the support mailbox.

On Android, GrowFit leaves all app data out of cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Your privacy rights

You can view, export and delete your workout records yourself in the app, because they are stored on your device.

Creatrix cannot see, correct or delete these on-device records for you, because it does not receive them.

To object to usage analytics, turn off “App usage stats” in Settings.

For other requests, such as access to, deletion of or objection to crash report or usage data, or a complaint about how we handle your data, email creatrix.studio26+growfit@gmail.com.

We answer requests to access, correct or delete your personal data or to stop its processing within the time the law sets: 10 days under Korean law, and one month under the GDPR, which can be extended by two further months where necessary.

Data we receive is not linked to your name, email address or store account: usage events carry only a random number for each event, and Sentry tells sessions apart by its own installation ID, which the app does not show.

So we cannot find your data by name or email address, but if you tell us the approximate time you used the app, your device model and the app version, we will try to find the matching records and then show, delete or stop processing them, and if we cannot find them, we will tell you why.

If you are in Korea, you can also ask the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972, https://www.kopico.go.kr) or the Korea Internet & Security Agency’s Privacy Infringement Report Center (118, https://privacy.kisa.or.kr) for help.

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular where you live or work or where you think your rights were infringed.

The EU and EEA authorities are listed at https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en, and in the UK the authority is the Information Commissioner’s Office (https://ico.org.uk).

In Switzerland, you can complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Children’s privacy

GrowFit is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

If you think a child under 14 has used the app and data was sent to us or our providers, email creatrix.studio26+growfit@gmail.com and we will help delete it.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as needed to comply with relevant regulations and reflect any new practices.

This policy takes effect on 26 November 2026.

If we make a significant change, we update the effective date at the top of this page and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Privacy contact

If you have a question about this policy, please contact us at creatrix.studio26+growfit@gmail.com.
