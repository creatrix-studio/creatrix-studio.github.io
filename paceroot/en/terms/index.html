To provide the app, it needs permission to access these items and functions on your phone, for these reasons:

  • Apple Health and Health Connect workouts, distance and heart rate (read only): to read your past runs, with their distance and heart rate, and set your next run.
  • Notifications: for run-day reminders at the time you set and a reminder 2 days before the first payment.
  • Photos, add only (iPhone): to save the run image you choose to save.
  • Watch scheduling (iPhone, WorkoutKit): to put today's planned run on your Apple Watch, if you choose a watch.

You may decline any of these permissions, and the other features of the app still work. What changes depends on the permission. Without location you cannot record a run with your phone. Without access to workouts, the plan uses only the runs you record in the app. Without heart rate, runs come in without heart rate and the plan does not use it. Without notifications you get no reminders. Without photos you cannot save the run image. Without watch scheduling, today's run does not go to your Apple Watch.
