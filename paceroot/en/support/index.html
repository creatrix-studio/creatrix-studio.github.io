Subscription, free trial and restore

  • iPhone: Settings app, your name, Subscriptions, Paceroot, Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play app, your profile picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Paceroot, Cancel.
  • In the app, Settings, Subscription takes you there too. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
  • New phone: sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings, Subscription and tap Restore.
  • The free trial is 7 days. The purchase screen shows the trial and the price after it before you confirm. On the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. On Google Play, cancel before the trial ends. Then you are not charged. If notifications are on, the app schedules a reminder 2 days before the first payment; your phone decides whether it is shown.

How do I get a refund?

  • App Store: ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: ask through Google Play under its refund policy. Where its rules let a developer refund an order, we can help too, so email us.
  • If a store sends you to us, send us a note, tell us what is up, and we will work with you.

My runs from Apple Health or Health Connect don't show

Check that the app may read workouts, distance and heart rate. If only heart rate is off, runs still show but without heart rate, and the plan does not use it. iPhone: Health app, your profile picture, Apps, Paceroot. Android: Health Connect in the phone's settings, App permissions, Paceroot. Samsung Health and Galaxy Watch runs come in through Health Connect, so Samsung Health must also be allowed to write to Health Connect.

The plan looks wrong

On Today, open the reason for the plan and tap Report this plan. Your report is sent without your name or run data. Please do not write personal or health details in the text.

How do I delete my data?

Many of these rights can be exercised in the app, with no account. Settings, Delete my runs removes all runs, routes, badges and waiting plan reports from the app. To delete one run, open it in History and tap Delete run. Your subscription stays. Runs in Apple Health or Health Connect are not touched. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you can turn off usage data in Settings, Send usage data. See the Privacy Policy for more.
