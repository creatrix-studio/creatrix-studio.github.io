Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-22

The privacy of your data, and it is your data, not ours, is a big deal to us. In this policy, we lay out: what data the app uses and why; how your data is handled; and your rights with respect to your data. We promise we never sell your data: never have, never will.

Our guiding principle is to collect only what we need.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to all products built and maintained by Creatrix ("we") including the Paceroot app (together, "the app"). The app has no account and shows no ads.

2. Your data on your phone

The app keeps these only in its own storage on your phone. We do not receive them, except the fixed event values listed in section 3 (your plan settings, and which screens you open, such as the level-up, streak and badge screens). How a run felt, and why a day was missed (pain or injury, not feeling well), never leave your phone.

  • Runs: date, type, distance, moving time, pace, heart rate when your source has it, and the route of runs you record with the phone.
  • How a run felt, and why you could not run on a day (pain or injury, not feeling well, traveling).
  • Your plan, badges and settings (runs per week, units, reminders, watch).
  • Runs read from Apple Health or Health Connect (including Samsung Health and Galaxy Watch through Health Connect): type, date, distance, duration and heart rate. The app only reads; it never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

Location: when you record a run with your phone, the app uses your precise location while the run is in progress, also with the screen locked, to measure distance and pace. The route is saved with the run on your phone. The app draws its own maps and does not send your location or routes to us or to a map service. On Android the position is provided by Google Play services on your device.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include this data if you have backup turned on. That backup is under your account, not ours. If you use an Apple Watch, the app puts today's planned run on the watch through Apple's own sync. Widgets and the Lock Screen run display read their data from the app's storage on your phone. A share image shows the date, run type, distance, time, pace and the plan's reason, with no map, and goes only where you send or save it.

Deleting: Settings, Delete my runs removes all runs, routes, badges and plan reports waiting to be sent; your subscription, units, reminders, watch choice, runs per week and usual distance stay. One run can be deleted from its own screen. Delete my runs does not change data in Apple Health or Health Connect, and does not cancel your subscription. Deleting the app removes everything except what is in your own phone backup.

3. Usage data and crash reports

We collect information about how the app is used for analytics and statistical purposes, to see which screens and steps people use and where they stop. The app sends pseudonymous product events, such as a screen opened, a run started or saved, a purchase started or completed, or a permission answered. Each event value comes from a fixed list.

Google Analytics for Firebase receives all events, plus these settings: device region, app language, subscription state (free, trial, paid, expired), notification permission, install week and install channel. Firebase also processes an app instance identifier (a random ID that is new for each install, so events can be told apart without your name or account), device model, OS and app version, and an approximate location that Google derives from your connection.

Our own server receives the key events (for example a run saved or a purchase) so we can count them reliably: the event name with its fixed values, the time, a random event number used to drop duplicates, the app and OS versions and the platform. It does not store your IP address and has no user or device ID; it reads the IP address only in memory, to limit repeated sending. Events wait on your phone until they can be sent (at most 500 events, 30 days).

When the app crashes, freezes or is slow, it sends a report through Sentry: the error type and where in the code it happened, device model and maker, OS and app version, how long app start and screens took, requests to our own server (path, status and duration), and a trail of recent screens and app events. Every product event is also added to that trail, so the fixed event values can appear in a crash report even when usage data is off. Before sending, the app removes error messages, thread names, web addresses, IP addresses and host names. Crash reports carry no distance, time, pace, heart rate, location or route. Sentry also records a session on every launch (start, end and whether the app crashed) with its own random install ID, and about 5% of launches are traced for app start and screen timings. Screenshots, screen recording and view captures are off.

Some fixed event values describe your plan: runs per week, watch or phone, reminders on or off, units, your level (beginner or runner), and the training section you complete and the step screen you open. We never send how a run felt, pain, injury or sickness, in an event or in a crash report: after a run the app records only that you answered, never the answer, and on a day you cannot run only the choice traveling is sent, while pain or injury and not feeling well send nothing. No distance, time, pace, heart rate, location, route, run date or text you typed is ever in an event, but the time an event is sent is recorded. We turned off advertising features. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android) or the iOS vendor identifier for analytics.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (by your device's region setting): the app asks before sending usage data. If you choose Don't allow, it sends none. If you choose Allow, it sends it. You can turn usage data off at any time in Settings, Send usage data. The legal basis for usage data there is your consent.

Everywhere else, including Korea: usage data is on by default, and Settings, Send usage data turns it off at any time; that is how you refuse or object. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in seeing how the app is used and improving it (Korean Personal Information Protection Act article 15(1)(6)). Data we have already received stays until its retention period ends (section 10).

Crash reports are sent in every region, with no consent screen and no switch. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in finding and fixing crashes (GDPR article 6(1)(f); Korean Personal Information Protection Act article 15(1)(6)). They contain no health data and no user content. You can object at any time by writing to creatrix.studio26+paceroot@gmail.com (GDPR article 21).

4. Plan reports you send

If you tap Report this plan and send it, we keep that report, including the reason you chose and any text you wrote, so that we have a history of reports to reference when we fix plans that feel wrong. We also receive the app version, the platform, the OS version and your device's language setting. No account, e-mail address, device ID or run data is sent. We add no ID to a report, so we cannot reply to it. What you type yourself could identify you, so please do not write personal or health details. If the phone is offline, the report waits on the phone and is sent later.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and free trials are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Your payment information is submitted directly to the store and never reaches us. The store tells the app on your phone whether your subscription is active, in a trial, and when it renews. The app uses this to unlock features and to remind you 2 days before the first payment, with a notification scheduled on your phone.

6. Permissions and notifications

The app asks for your consent before it uses your location, reads your workouts, distance and heart rate from Apple Health or Health Connect, saves a share image to your photos (iPhone), schedules runs on your Apple Watch, or sends notifications. Consent is always optional and the app will function without it, though some features, such as recording a run with your phone, may be unavailable. The rating prompt is the system's own; the app is not told what you answer.

Run-day reminders and the payment reminder are scheduled on your phone. We do not use a push service.

8. Where your data goes

Our server is operated in the United States. If you are located in the European Union, UK, or elsewhere outside of the United States, please be aware that any information the app sends to us will be transferred to and stored in the United States. Google and Sentry also process data in the United States and, for Google, other locations.

9. How we secure your data

All data is encrypted via TLS when it is transmitted from the app to our servers and to our providers.

10. Data retention

We keep your information for the time necessary for the purposes for which it is processed. We may also retain and use this information as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Through this policy, we have provided specific retention periods for certain types of information:

  • On your phone: until you delete it (section 2).
  • Key events and plan reports on our server: deleted within a week after they are 1 year old, by a weekly job.
  • Usage data at Google: 2 months. Crash reports at Sentry: 90 days.

11. Your rights

We strive to apply the same data rights to everyone, regardless of location. Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how it is processed, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to creatrix.studio26+paceroot@gmail.com. We reply within 10 days.

Data we receive is not linked to your name or account. Usage data and crash reports are told apart by separate random install IDs (the Firebase app instance ID and Sentry's own install ID), and server events carry only a random event number. We cannot find your data by name or e-mail address. If you can point us to it, for example with the approximate time you sent something and your app version, we will help you find it and then show, delete or stop processing it. If we cannot find it, we will tell you why.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. If you are in the EU or UK, you can contact your data protection authority to file a complaint or learn more about local privacy laws.

12. Children

Paceroot is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as needed to comply with relevant regulations and reflect any new practices. Whenever we make a significant change to our policies, we will refresh the effective date at the top of this page and take any other appropriate steps to notify you.
