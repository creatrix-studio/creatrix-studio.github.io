Snore Native Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-15

Your data belongs to you. In this policy, we lay out what data is processed and why, how it is handled, and your rights. This policy applies to Snore Native, its support pages and correspondence with Creatrix. Contact: creatrix.studio26@gmail.com.

Recordings and health-related inputs stay on your device unless you choose to share or export them. This does not mean there is no off-device collection: advertising and optional analytics SDKs process the service data described below.

Collection · Disclosure · Your rights · Security · Deletion · Retention · Location · Transfers · Changes

What we collect and why

Identity and access

Snore Native does not require an app account, email login or account sync. Optional profile details and questionnaire answers are stored locally, not submitted as an account registration. Local attribution storage holds app-generated identifiers and sanitized referral information; the app does not supply those local anonymous, event or recording-session identifiers as Google Analytics event parameters. SDKs can generate their own identifiers, so the absence of an account does not make all service data anonymous.

Billing information

Apple App Store and Google Play handle payments through StoreKit and Play Billing. The app receives purchase and subscription entitlement information to check Premium access, restore purchases and maintain local entitlement state. It does not collect card numbers, bank details or billing addresses. When usage analytics is enabled, allowed events include purchase attempts, verification outcomes and plan categories, without receipts or payment details in custom event parameters. Firebase Analytics may also automatically measure in-app purchase activity when enabled. The stores retain their own transaction records under their policies.

Product interactions and local content

The app stores microphone audio, recording times, sound level samples and activity summaries, notes, tags, pins, optional weight and rested ratings, profile details, adult questionnaire answers, alarm choices and settings in app-private storage. Sound activity summaries are calculated on the device from recorded loudness levels; older stored analysis results remain local too. The app has no Creatrix-operated upload, account-sync or cloud-backup service for this content. Its recording data directory is excluded from system backup; Android app backup is disabled.

The app’s custom ad and analytics code does not send audio, sound levels, scores, health or questionnaire answers, profile values or free-text notes to providers. It does not automatically collect your contacts, photos, messages, browsing history or precise GPS location. A share or export that you initiate can send selected content to an app or destination you choose. Settings export includes session summaries, notes, tags, questionnaire answers and settings; audio is shared separately from a recording. Recipients’ handling of those copies is outside the app’s local storage controls.

Optional Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4): usage analytics is off by default. If you enable it, Google receives allowlisted app actions and outcomes, screen/feature categories, sanitized referral metadata and app/device information. Google’s SDK can process app-instance or device identifiers, IP-derived region and diagnostics, and automatic purchase measurements. Custom events describe actions rather than private values; for example, a profile edit can be logged without the profile content. The app disables automatic screen reporting and analytics advertising purposes; iOS IDFA/IDFV analytics collection and Android advertising-ID analytics collection are disabled. These settings do not eliminate all SDK identifiers or diagnostics.

Optional Microsoft Clarity: public-screen replay is a separate choice, also off by default. If enabled, Clarity receives interaction events and reconstructed screen layout for the Premium paywall, subscription help and acknowledgments, along with device/app information, coarse region, performance/error information and SDK-generated user/session identifiers or other usage data. App windows are masked by default, public elements may be unmasked, and replay is paused on private screens, recording and native payment flows. The app does not supply a personal identity to Clarity. These are capture restrictions, not a claim that every SDK-generated datum is anonymous or that providers never receive diagnostics.

General geolocation data

Network providers receive IP addresses when their services are contacted. AdMob uses IP addresses to estimate general location, and optional Google Analytics and Clarity can process approximate region. The app does not request precise location permission or supply GPS coordinates to these SDKs.

Website interactions

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics, advertising or replay scripts, tracking pixels or embedded forms added by Creatrix. GitHub processes visitors’ connection information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following external links exposes connection information to those destinations under their policies.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising and ad-consent choices. The app starts an ad request from the explicit ad-supported recording flow, checks UMP consent status and requests a non-personalized ad. This consent process is separate from optional usage analytics and replay consent.

AdMob can collect and share IP/general location, app- or device/account identifiers, advertisements seen, ad and app interactions, crash logs and performance or other diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and SDK operation. UMP processes consent choices and provider/device/request context and its SDK disclosures include coarse location, interactions and performance. The app does not supply recordings, scores, questionnaire/profile values, custom audiences or location targeting. A non-personalized request still involves service data and identifiers.

The current iOS app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or read IDFA, and Android removes the classic advertising-ID permission. These facts do not establish that every provider practice is nontracking: Google’s bundled iOS ad SDK declares a device-identifier category for tracking, and other app/device identifiers or ad-service capabilities may remain. We therefore do not promise “no tracking” or “no identifiers.” See Google’s Privacy Policy, the AdMob disclosures for iOS and Android, Firebase privacy information and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

These pages do not set Creatrix cookies. In the native app, preferences, consent and entitlement state are stored locally; third-party SDK storage and identifiers are governed by the relevant service settings and policies.

Voluntary correspondence

When you email Creatrix with a question or ask for help, we receive your email address and whatever message, attachments or diagnostic details you choose to send. We use that correspondence to respond and investigate the issue, and may keep it as a history of the request. The app does not automatically attach your recordings or health inputs. Please send only information needed for your request.

How we approach mobile app permissions

Microphone permission is needed to record sound. Notification and alarm permissions support the reminders and alarms you choose, including relevant Android exact-alarm access and iOS AlarmKit permission where used. You can change permissions in your operating-system settings; declining a permission can disable the corresponding feature. The current app does not connect to Apple Health or Health Connect to upload or sync health data.

When we access or disclose your information

To provide services you request: Google handles ads and consent, Google/Firebase and Microsoft handle the optional analytics you enable, Apple and Google handle store transactions, and GitHub hosts these pages. They receive the service data described above and may process it under their own terms. We do not have remote access to your local recordings.

At your direction: exports, system sharing and email send content only when you initiate the action. For troubleshooting, we work with information you voluntarily provide; contact us before sending sensitive files. We do not sell your local recordings or health inputs or provide them for ad targeting.

To protect rights or comply with law: we may preserve or disclose information actually in our possession when necessary to comply with applicable legal process or to address unlawful use, fraud or threats to safety. We cannot disclose local content we have never received. A business transfer involving information we hold would require applicable legal safeguards and notice. There is no blanket permission to transfer your local recordings.

Your rights with respect to your information

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to know about processing, access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a privacy authority. Applicable rights relating to sale/sharing, automated decisions, non-discrimination or an appeal also remain available. We do not use the app’s sound summaries to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. Some information may be exempt from a request under applicable law.

View, edit, export or delete local content in the app. In Settings → Privacy and local data → Change analytics choices, you can independently change usage analytics and public-screen replay. Turning them off disables future optional collection; it does not delete information already received by providers or stop separate store/ad processing. Advertising privacy choices are available in that screen when UMP requires them; device and provider settings offer additional controls.

For information held by us or help with a provider request, email creatrix.studio26@gmail.com. We may need reasonable proof of identity or an agent’s authority and enough information to locate data. No app account links every provider identifier to your email, so we may not be able to identify all SDK records. We will explain available steps and applicable limits rather than promise erasure we cannot verify. You may also contact Apple, Google, Microsoft or GitHub under their privacy policies.

How we secure your data

Recordings and related local data use the operating system’s app sandbox and backup exclusions described above. The app does not add its own encryption layer to every audio file. Keep your device secured and updated; anyone with access to an unlocked device or an exported file may be able to read its contents. Providers document security measures such as encrypted transport for their services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and this policy does not promise universal end-to-end encryption or protection from every loss.

What happens when you delete content

Deleting a completed session removes its local audio, associated notes and session metadata. Routine “Delete unpinned recordings” cleanup preserves pinned sessions and active recordings; delete pinned sessions individually if you want to remove them. Audio retention modes can discard unselected audio segments while keeping pins. Removed audio cannot be restored by us.

To remove remaining local profile, questionnaire and settings data, delete the app on iOS rather than offloading it, or clear app storage on Android. Export anything you need first. There is no cloud account to delete or recover from. Local deletion does not cancel a subscription, erase copies shared elsewhere, or erase store transactions or provider telemetry.

Data retention

Local data remains until you delete it, remove app storage, or apply the selected audio-retention settings. We keep support information for the time necessary to handle the request and meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or enforce agreements. There is no fixed support-retention period promised here.

Google, Microsoft, Apple and GitHub retain service records under their own policies and configured service settings. We do not promise a particular SDK retention period or automatic provider deletion after uninstalling. Contact us for assistance with a request; provider retention and deletion scope may differ from local deletion.

Location of site and data

Private recording content is processed on your device. Data sent to Google, Microsoft, Apple, GitHub or an email provider may be processed outside your country, including in the United States and other countries where those providers operate. We do not claim that all data stays in Korea or in a particular provider region.

International transfers

Applicable privacy and international-transfer requirements remain in force, including any required notices, legal grounds, consent or safeguards for information sent abroad. Provider policies describe their transfer practices. We do not claim that Creatrix has adopted Basecamp’s data processing agreement or its contractual transfer arrangements, and use of the app is not a substitute for any consent separately required by law. Contact us for information relevant to your request.

Children

Snore Native is designed for adults and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and information was sent to a provider, contact creatrix.studio26@gmail.com and we will help remove it where we can.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy as needed to comply with relevant regulations and reflect new practices. Whenever we make a significant change, we will refresh the date at the top of this page and take appropriate steps to notify users, including obtaining consent where required.

Have questions, comments or concerns about this policy, your data or your rights? Email creatrix.studio26@gmail.com. The English version prevails if translations differ, except where Korean law requires otherwise.
