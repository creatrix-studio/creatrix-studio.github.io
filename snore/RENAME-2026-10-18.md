# Nightclip naming decision — October 18, 2026

The owner approved renaming Snore Native to **Nightclip** (Korean: **나이트클립**). The owner reports preliminary USPTO, KIPRIS, App Store and Google Play checks found no conflicts. These preliminary checks are not legal clearance and were not independently repeated by this task.

The English and Korean support, privacy and terms pages use the new product name in visible copy, page titles and descriptions. Existing URLs, link targets, legal entity, policy dates, layout and substantive policy text remain unchanged. The encoded support email subject in its existing URL is intentionally preserved. Native app/store drafts are maintained in `coding/projects/snore-native`; identifiers remain `com.creatrix.snorenative`.

This is a local commit only. Publication/push and store-console edits remain pending owner action. Old screenshot/feature-graphic text was outside this text-only rename. Static HTML checks verify the six pages and unchanged URL attributes; no browser or device runtime validation is claimed.
