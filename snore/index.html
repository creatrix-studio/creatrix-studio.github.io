Snorbit

Record tonight. Hear it in the morning.

Put your phone by your bed and tap Start recording. In the morning, hear the loudest saved clip, see your 0–100 sound score and compare nights. Send a clip to your partner only if you choose. Your first recording is free, with no paywall before you start. No Snorbit account is required.

Snorbit is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat snoring, sleep apnea or any other condition. It cannot detect sleep apnea. Its recordings and summaries are for personal reference, not medical advice. A quiet night does not rule out a health concern. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, purchase help or privacy requests, email creatrix.studio26@gmail.com. Include your phone model, operating system and app version when reporting a problem. Send recordings or health information only if you choose to share them.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start tonight?

Open Tonight and tap Start recording. Allow microphone access when asked, and leave the phone by your bed with enough power and storage. When you wake up, hold “I’m up” for about a second to finish. The first recording is free. Options such as alarms and recording settings are under More.

What does the morning screen mean?

Play the loudest saved moment first. If there is no loud clip, the screen says so. The 0–100 sound score describes recorded sound: higher means louder sound for longer. Noisy night means at least 10 minutes of sound above the app’s background threshold; Quiet night means less than 10 minutes. These minutes are sound time, not snoring time or time asleep. The microphone can pick up other people and background sounds. It cannot tell who made a sound. See details opens the full timeline and saved clips. No ad or paywall blocks the morning clip and summary.

How does the two-night test work?

After a noisy night, start a two-night test from the morning screen. Try the suggested change, such as sleeping on your side, on your next recorded night. The next result compares sound time before and after. “Better”, “About the same” and “Worse” refer to sound time, not your health. A difference between two nights does not prove what caused it or promise improvement. You can compare the next night you are able to record; the free cycle still applies.

How do I send a clip to my partner?

Tap Send to partner on the morning screen to open your phone’s share sheet with the clip and a one-line summary. Choose an app and recipient, or cancel. The app does not send anything automatically. Once sent, the recipient may keep a copy; deleting the recording here does not delete that copy.

Does Ask AI send my data anywhere?

No. Ask AI prepares text on your phone; it does not contact an AI service. Open it from History or See details, choose your records and review the summary before copying or sharing it. History offers the last 7 saved nights, last 30 saved nights or all nights; these are record counts, not calendar days. Notes, profile details and questionnaire answers are excluded by default. The export never includes audio, file paths or identifiers. Nothing is sent to an AI service unless you share or paste it there. The service you choose then handles it under its own privacy rules. AI output is not a diagnosis.

Where are my recordings stored?

Recordings, sound summaries, notes and health-related inputs stay in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not upload them to a Creatrix server or include them in ads or analytics. Files leave the app when you explicitly share or export them. There is no account sync or app cloud backup. Ads and optional analytics have separate data practices described in the privacy policy.

How does the free-night cycle work?

The four-night cycle is: night 1 free, night 2 after an ad, night 3 Premium only, night 4 rest. Then it repeats. The rest night pauses free recording; Premium is not subject to this cycle. Tonight shows all four steps in a strip and highlights the current step, so you can see what comes next. No paywall appears before your first recording. Ad availability and consent can affect the ad step; follow the option shown in the app if an ad is unavailable.

How do I restore Premium?

Open More → Premium or More → Settings → Subscription help and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account or Google Play account used for the subscription and connect to the internet. Restore checks an active entitlement with that store; it does not restore deleted recordings or transfer a purchase between Apple and Google.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Snorbit → Cancel Subscription. On Android, open Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Snorbit → Cancel subscription. Deleting the app or emailing support does not cancel billing. Access normally continues until the end of the paid period, subject to the store’s rules. See Apple’s cancellation help or Google Play’s cancellation help.

How do refunds work?

Apple and Google handle purchases and refund decisions under their policies and applicable consumer law. Request an Apple refund at Report a Problem, or follow Google Play’s refund instructions.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a completed session from History to remove its local audio and associated notes and metadata. More → Settings → Audio storage → Delete unpinned recordings removes completed, unpinned sessions; pinned sessions need to be deleted individually. To remove the remaining local profile, questionnaire and settings data, delete the app on iOS (not Offload App), or clear its app storage on Android. Export anything you want to keep first. Copies you shared and data already held by SDK providers are separate; contact us about provider data requests. Deleting local data does not cancel a subscription.

Can I change analytics and advertising choices?

Usage analytics and public-screen replay are separate choices, both off by default. Change them in More → Settings → Privacy and local data → Change analytics choices. Advertising privacy choices appear there when Google’s consent system requires them. Turning optional analytics off stops future optional collection; it does not erase data previously received by a provider.

What should I check before recording?

Allow microphone access, leave enough storage and power, and obtain any consent required from everyone who may be recorded. You are responsible for following local recording laws. Phone placement, background sounds and interruptions can affect results; a sound summary cannot establish whether you have a medical condition.
