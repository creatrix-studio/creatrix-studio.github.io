Snore Native

Record and review the sounds of your night

Snore Native uses your phone’s microphone to record nighttime sounds, lets you listen back, and shows sound activity summaries calculated on your device. You can keep notes, tag nights and pin recordings. No Snore Native account is required.

Snore Native is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat snoring, sleep apnea or any other condition. Its recordings and summaries are for personal reference, not medical advice. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, purchase help or privacy requests, email creatrix.studio26@gmail.com. Include your phone model, operating system and app version when reporting a problem. Send recordings or health information only if you choose to share them.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my recordings stored?

Recordings, sound summaries, notes and health-related inputs stay in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not upload them to a Creatrix server or include them in ads or analytics. Files leave the app when you explicitly share or export them. There is no account sync or app cloud backup. Ads and optional analytics have separate data practices described in the privacy policy.

How does the free-night cycle work?

The four-night cycle is: night 1 free, night 2 after an ad, night 3 Premium only, night 4 rest. Then it repeats. The rest night pauses free recording; Premium is not subject to this cycle. The Home screen shows the access available tonight. Ad availability and consent can affect the ad step; follow the option shown in the app if an ad is unavailable.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen or Settings → Subscription help and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account or Google Play account used for the subscription and connect to the internet. Restore checks an active entitlement with that store; it does not restore deleted recordings or transfer a purchase between Apple and Google.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Snore Native → Cancel Subscription. On Android, open Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Snore Native → Cancel subscription. Deleting the app or emailing support does not cancel billing. Access normally continues until the end of the paid period, subject to the store’s rules. See Apple’s cancellation help or Google Play’s cancellation help.

How do refunds work?

Apple and Google handle purchases and refund decisions under their policies and applicable consumer law. Request an Apple refund at Report a Problem, or follow Google Play’s refund instructions.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a completed session from recording history to remove its local audio and associated notes and metadata. Settings → Audio storage → Delete unpinned recordings removes completed, unpinned sessions; pinned sessions need to be deleted individually. To remove the remaining local profile, questionnaire and settings data, delete the app on iOS (not Offload App), or clear its app storage on Android. Export anything you want to keep first. Copies you shared and data already held by SDK providers are separate; contact us about provider data requests. Deleting local data does not cancel a subscription.

Can I change analytics and advertising choices?

Usage analytics and public-screen replay are separate choices, both off by default. Change them in Settings → Privacy and local data → Change analytics choices. Advertising privacy choices appear there when Google’s consent system requires them. Turning optional analytics off stops future optional collection; it does not erase data previously received by a provider.

What should I check before recording?

Allow microphone access, leave enough storage and power, and obtain any consent required from everyone who may be recorded. You are responsible for following local recording laws. Phone placement, background sounds and interruptions can affect results; a sound summary cannot establish whether you have a medical condition.
